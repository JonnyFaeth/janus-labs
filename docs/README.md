# Janus Labs project notes

## Direction and audience hypothesis

The established direction is AI-native scientific intelligence infrastructure for quantum experimentation. A proposed first audience is researchers who need to connect evidence, planning notes, and result interpretation. The specific discipline, laboratory workflow, and integration requirements remain to be validated.

## Proposed first scope

A fictional question-to-result record that illustrates provenance and human review. It should expose reasoning gaps without asserting that a generated experiment or interpretation is scientifically valid.

## Open questions

- Which step consumes the most avoidable coordination effort?
- What source attribution is sufficient for review?
- How should conflicting evidence and unsupported suggestions be shown?
- What would distinguish a useful research aid from an additional note-taking burden?

## Non-goals

No laboratory hardware control, autonomous experimental execution, new quantum algorithm, validated discovery, or performance advantage is claimed.

## Publication boundaries

Recommend public visibility for high-level concept documents and synthetic demonstrations. Keep proprietary architecture, detailed experimental protocols, unpublished data, restricted papers, partner agreements, credentials, and instrument access information private and outside this repository.

Publish only research material with appropriate sharing rights. A public folder cannot be made private by its name or by an ignore rule. Any future real-data pilot needs an explicit confidentiality and provenance review.
