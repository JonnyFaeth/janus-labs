# Janus Labs

**AI-native scientific intelligence infrastructure for quantum experimentation.**

Janus Labs explores a workspace that connects scientific questions, evidence, experiment planning, and results. Its intended role is to help researchers navigate the reasoning around an experiment while keeping provenance and human review visible.

## Status

**Concept / discovery.** This repository documents a proposed direction. It does not contain a working research platform, hardware integration, validated scientific result, or autonomous experiment system.

## The problem

An experiment's reasoning can become scattered across papers, notes, plans, and result files. Janus Labs proposes a connected record of what was asked, what evidence informed the plan, what was observed, and what remains uncertain.

## Proposed workflow

1. Frame a research question and its assumptions.
2. Organize relevant, permitted sources and researcher notes.
3. Draft an experiment brief for human review.
4. Associate a result summary with the question and approved plan.
5. Record interpretation, limitations, and possible next questions.

AI-assisted suggestions would be labeled as suggestions and remain subject to researcher review. The initial demonstration would use a fictional experiment; it would not operate laboratory equipment.

## Design principles

- Preserve provenance from a claim back to its supporting source.
- Distinguish observations, hypotheses, and generated suggestions.
- Make uncertainty and conflicting evidence explicit.
- Keep researchers responsible for scientific judgments and approvals.
- Treat reproducibility as something to demonstrate, not assume.

Read the project notes and roadmap. There is no application to install yet. Useful feedback includes gaps in evidence traceability, experiment review, and interpretation workflows.

## Visibility and licensing

**Recommendation: public concept showcase; private research and implementation.** Detailed experimental protocols, unpublished results, partner information, proprietary orchestration, and hardware access details are omitted. Keep restricted research outside this repository.

No open-source license has been selected. No scientific performance or discovery claim is made by these planning documents.
